## Sample art

The "Phosphor Terminal" sample (`scripts/make_phosphor_terminal_theme.py`,
`samples/phosphor-terminal.n6theme`) is drawn procedurally by that script. The "Rock Theme"
sample (`samples/rock-theme/`, `samples/rock-theme.n6theme`) is original AI-generated artwork
made for this project. Neither uses any logo, artwork, font or other asset from a third party,
and neither is affiliated with or endorsed by any band, game or brand. The AI test sheet in
`tests/fixtures/` was generated by sharphatman-ux with Google AI.
